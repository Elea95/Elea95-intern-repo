# Help Strategy - When to Use Google, AI, or Ask for Help? #55

## When do you prefer using AI vs. searching Google?

- AI is great for quick syntax help, explanations, and boilerplate code.
- Google is better for **official documentation, Stack Overflow**, and real-world solutions.

## How do you decide when to ask a colleague instead?

- If AI and Google fail, OR
- If the problem is **specific to the project/team**, OR
- If it's a **time-sensitive critical issue**.

## What challenges do developers face when troubleshooting alone?

- **Information overload** (too many conflicting answers).
- **Debugging blind spots** (hard to see own mistakes).
- **AI hallucinations** (AI generates incorrect or misleading answers).
